## Who we are

The Ultimate DIY Hub is a personal how-to site publishing DIY and home repair guides. This
policy explains what data is collected when you visit, and what you can do about it.

## Cookies and advertising

We use **Google AdSense** to serve advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to
serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising
cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site
and/or other sites on the internet.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads. You can opt out of
personalised advertising by visiting
[Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can also opt out of some
third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting
[www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

## Analytics

We use **Google Analytics 4** to understand how visitors use the site — for example which
pages are read most. GA4 records page views and usage events. It does not receive your name,
email address, or anything else that directly identifies you.

You can prevent Analytics from recognising you on return visits by installing the
[Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## What we do not do

- We do not run a comment system, so we never receive your comments or account details.
- We do not operate a newsletter or mailing list.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade any information about visitors.

## Log data

Like most websites, our host records standard request information — IP address, browser
type, referring page and timestamp. This is used only to diagnose faults and to measure
traffic, and is not combined with anything else.

## Children's privacy

This site is written for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The date at the top of this page reflects
the most recent revision.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the repository that publishes this site.
